## How to Use These Forms

### Circle Community Pulse
1. Create the form from the structure above
2. Share link via WhatsApp broadcast to your member list
3. After responses come in:
   - Go to Responses → Linked Sheets
   - Copy the sheet tab and rename it "Q1 2026 Pulse"
   - Leave the original tab as a template for next quarter
4. Repeat quarterly — each quarter gets its own clean sheet

### Testimonials
1. Create the form
2. Trigger it when:
   - Someone gives NPS 9–10 in the Pulse
   - Someone writes a positive answer in "How has BR helped you?" and checked the testimonial box
   - You ask directly after a circle
3. Use the "Share form" link to send directly to those people — don't post the link publicly
