---

On March 22nd we are joining forces with The Gathering of Men for a massive day: **The Spirit of Pamoja**.

**Pamoja** — a Swahili word meaning "together" or "unity" — its what emerges when men gather in true brotherhood - a state we arrive at when we show up fully for ourselves and each other.

---

**We're skipping the theory. We jump straight into action.**

This is not a workshop where you sit and listen. This is an immersive experience where you move, feel, confront, and discover. Where you test your edges and find your center.

We'll be moving through 4 pillars:

**Peace**  
Calmness within.

**Power**  
Physical and mental strength.

**Purpose**  
Clarity and direction.

**Play**  
Approaching life lightly.

---

We'll test ourselves like warriors, find stillness like monks, discover purpose like kings, and laugh freely like drunken masters.

Expect raw moments, confronting resistance, honest conversations, sweat, moments to rest and reflect and laughter.

**That is the true spirit of being together > Pamoja.**

- Test ourselves like **warriors**
- Find stillness like **monks**
- Discover purpose like **kings**
- Laugh freely like **drunken masters**
